ZET trams and buses live in Zagreb

A public transport map with live vehicle locations, stops and timetables. Share the tram or bus you are on with a single link.

Open the map

ZET Web is an unofficial web application for tracking public transport in Zagreb. It shows live locations of ZET trams and buses on a map, along with stops and line timetables. If you are wondering “where is my tram” or “when is the bus coming”, or you want to know how far a vehicle is from your stop, this gives you a clear answer.

The app is not affiliated with ZET or the City of Zagreb. It uses publicly available data and displays it in your browser.

What you can do in the app

  • Track trams and buses live on a map of Zagreb, with clearly marked lines.
  • Find tram and bus stops anywhere in the city.
  • Check line timetables before departure or transfers.
  • Share the tram or bus you are on — click the vehicle, tap “Share” and send the link.
  • See Bajs bike stations (bike rental) alongside public transport — Official site: Bajs Zagreb.

Why this is more useful than a timetable alone

A timetable shows the plan, but real traffic often differs. This map shows how ZET trams and buses actually move through the city — including delays and gaps between vehicles.

When someone is waiting for you or you are on the move, it is enough to send a link to the vehicle. No extra explanations and no guessing where someone is.

How to use it

  1. Open the map.
  2. Zoom to the area or stop you are interested in.
  3. Click a tram or bus to see the line and details.
  4. To share: click “Share” and send the copied link.

Data source and accuracy

Vehicle locations come from publicly available GTFS Realtime data published by ZET. The app refreshes this data automatically and displays it with minimal delay, but accuracy always depends on the quality of the source — occasional discrepancies or outages are possible.

Frequently asked questions

Is this an official ZET app?
No. This is an unofficial app that uses publicly available data.
How accurate is the tram and bus data?
The display is near real time, but it may occasionally lag or differ depending on the data source.
How can I share the location of a tram or bus?
Click on a vehicle on the map, choose “Share” and send the copied link via any chat app.
Do I need to install the app?
No. It works directly in your browser on mobile and desktop.
Does it show Bajs bike stations?
Yes. You can also see Bajs bike rental stations alongside public transport stops.

This application is not affiliated with ZET, the City of Zagreb or any other official institution. It is an unofficial project based on publicly available data.
